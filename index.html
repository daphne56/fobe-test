Friends of Blenman‑Elm (FOBE) is a nonprofit dedicated to preserving, protecting, and enhancing the character, livability, and historic and cultural integrity of the Blenman‑Elm neighborhood — a safe, clean place to call home.

About FOBE

Rooted in the neighborhood.

FOBE exists so that Blenman‑Elm stays a place people are proud to live in — its architecture respected, its streets safe, and its character intact for the neighbors who come after us.

We are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit corporation organized under the laws of the State of Arizona. Membership is open to anyone who cares about the neighborhood's future.
